// embeddings and search

Search that understands images and text

We take an embedding model from raw data to a production service: label and clean the data, fine-tune the model, host it reliably, and put it behind the search your product runs on.

01

Label and annotate

Annotation guidelines, labelled pairs and spot checks, so the training data says what you mean.

02

Clean and verify

Dedupe, consistency checks and a verification pass before anything reaches training.

03

Fine-tune

Qwen and GME embedding models trained on your data for text, image and mixed queries.

04

Serve

Stable hosting on EC2 or SageMaker, packaged as production-ready pods with health checks.

05

Search

Vectors in a vector database, queried by your app through one search API.

embeddings · podsec2 · sagemaker
